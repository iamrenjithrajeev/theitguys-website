Storage Keys · Equipment plan

What we install, and which brands.

Everything the system needs to work like the demo: the smart padlocks, what connects them, the gate, cameras and the office. For each item we show the brand we recommend and where you can save money without lowering security.

Will the plan work?

Yes, with three changes to what the demo shows today.

  • Unlock by card at the unit door needs the premium lock. Only Nokē has a card reader on the unit lock. With the value lock (igloohome), customers open their unit with the app or a PIN, and the card works at the gate only.
  • Live logs and remote unlock need a connection box near every lock. The padlocks work offline, but the admin panel only sees an unlock straight away, and can only open a lock remotely, if a gateway or bridge is in range. We plan these per corridor.
  • Fire mode must be wired, not only in the app. When the fire alarm goes off, the gate and exit doors must open on their own, through a relay from the fire panel, even if the internet or the app is down. The app then shows and logs it. Unit padlocks stay locked, as in the demo.

Cheap TTLock-based padlocks

Lowest price · many brand names
Why not
Security researchers have published serious flaws in the TTLock system: a fixed encryption key built into the app and locks, expired PINs that can be made to work again by faking the lock's clock, and pairing keys that can be captured over Bluetooth.
Watch for
These locks are sold under many different brand names. Before buying any cheap smart padlock, we check which app and platform it uses.

How we decide: lock strength (shackle grade), how the electronics are protected, whether it still opens when the battery or internet fails, and whether every unlock is logged. Price comes after these.

Lower cost, same security

Where you can save

  • igloohome instead of Nokē for the unit locks, if card access at the unit door is not needed.
  • TP-Link Omada instead of Ubiquiti for the network.
  • 2 MP cameras in short corridors, 4 MP only at entrances and lifts.
  • Card and PIN terminal at the gate instead of face recognition, if the owner accepts it.
  • Fewer bridges: live logs at the busiest corridors, offline PIN logs elsewhere.

Where not to save

  • No cheap TTLock-based padlocks. Known, published security flaws.
  • No weak hasps. The mesh door and hasp must be as strong as the lock.
  • No fire mode through the app only. It must be wired to the fire alarm panel.
  • No cameras on the guest Wi-Fi or open to the internet without protection.
  • No single internet line for the gate.

What we confirm at the site survey

  • Number of units per floor, and the first-floor units built to order.
  • Whether the owner needs card access at the unit door (decides igloohome or Nokē).
  • Sample locks and hasps tested on a real mesh door.
  • Signal checks along each corridor to decide where bridges and Wi-Fi go.
  • Requirements from Sharjah Civil Defence for fire mode, and for CCTV approval and recording time.
