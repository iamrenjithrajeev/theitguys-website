Storage Keys · Equipment plan

What we install, and which models.

Every unit door gets a smart lock fixed to the frame, a keypad reader and a door sensor, run by Hikvision door controllers. The gate and lifts use Hikvision face terminals. The Storage Keys app controls all of it and keeps every log in one place.

Will the plan work?

Yes. This is what we checked against Hikvision's own datasheets, manuals and integration documents.

✓Fixed lock on every unit door. No padlock to cut or remove. The lock stays locked in a power cut.
✓Open any door from the app. The controllers accept remote door control from our software (ISAPI and ISUP 5.0).
✓Face registered from the app. The customer's selfie is sent to the face terminals with their access dates. They walk up and the gate opens.
✓Card, PIN, QR code and Bluetooth at each unit door with the DS-K1105EMKB-QR reader.
✓Time-limited PINs from the app. Each person can have a PIN with a start and end time. Newer firmware lets the platform set PINs ("Platform-Applied Personal PIN"). For a single-use PIN, our system deletes it after the first opening.
✓Live door status and "left open" alerts. The controller has a door-sensor input per door and raises door-not-secured and forced-open alarms, which the app turns into notifications.
✓Works when the internet is down. The controllers work offline and keep their data when power is off. Face, card and PIN keep working. Events upload when the line is back.
✓All logs in one place. Every terminal and controller sends its events to our system: who, which door, when, how, allowed or refused, with the camera clip.
!Opening from the app when away needs internet. Everything at the door keeps working without it.
✕Not battery powered. Locks and controllers run on mains power. Each corridor cabinet needs a battery backup (UPS).

Do we need a gateway?

No separate gateway box. Hikvision devices sit on the facility network and talk to the Storage Keys server in one of two ways.

Recommended: ISUP 5.0

  • The device connects out to our server by itself, using a Device ID and key typed into the device once.
  • No PC on site and no port forwarding on the facility's router.
  • Supported by the DS-K1T342 face terminal and the DS-K2600T controllers.
  • The ISUP developer kit comes through Hikvision's Technology Partner Program. We register before development starts.

Alternative: ISAPI on the local network

  • Our software talks to each device by its IP address on the facility network.
  • Needs a small always-on PC or mini server on site to connect the devices to our cloud.
  • Fully documented by Hikvision, and good as a backup to ISUP.

Adding devices and doors later, without us

Try both in the demo admin panel: Devices at the top, and First floor → Set up a new unit.

A new device

  • Search the network to find Hikvision devices that aren't added yet, or type the device's IP address and its login.
  • Or use ISUP: the app shows a Device ID and key to type into the device's own settings.
  • Test connection reads the model, serial and what it can do, then adds it and syncs customers, faces and access rules.
  • Rule for buying new devices: the datasheet must list ISAPI or ISUP 5.0. Any Hikvision device that does is added the same way.

A door for a custom-built unit

  • 1. Unit size: width, depth and height give the square feet and suggested rent.
  • 2. Fit the door hardware: lock, keypad reader and door sensor, wired to the corridor cabinet.
  • 3. Add the door to the system: pick the controller and its free door number. Access and live alerts switch on.
  • 4. Test and go live: open from the app, check the door sensor, test a card and PIN, add an install photo.

Lower cost, same security

Where you can save

  • DS-K1107MK readers instead of DS-K1105EMKB-QR, if QR and Bluetooth at the unit door are not needed.
  • DS-K1T342MX face terminals without fingerprint.
  • DS-K2602T controllers where only two doors are close together.
  • 2 MP cameras in short corridors, 4 MP at the entrance and lifts.

Where not to save

  • No unit locks that open in a power cut. Unit doors must be fail-secure, and exit doors fail-safe.
  • No cabinets without battery backup.
  • No fire mode through the app only. It must be wired to the fire alarm panel.
  • No access devices on the guest Wi-Fi, and no device admin accounts used by the app.
  • No single internet line.

What we confirm before ordering

  • A sample kit on one real mesh door: lock, keypad reader, door sensor, controller and a face terminal, all controlled from our software.
  • The face terminal firmware has the Platform-Applied PIN mode, so PINs can come from the app.
  • Registration with Hikvision's Technology Partner Program for the ISUP developer kit.
  • Prices and stock from the Hikvision distributor in the UAE.
  • Sharjah Civil Defence requirements for fire mode, and CCTV approval and recording time.
  • Customer consent and a privacy notice for face data, which is sensitive personal data under UAE law. Faces are deleted when a customer leaves.
